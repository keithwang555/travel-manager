Appendix C: Critical Transportation & Child-Fare Nuances
Preschool Child Fare Rule (幼児 / Yōji) — Ethan Rides Local Trains Free:
Under Nankai Electric Railway, Osaka Metro, JR West, and Kintetsu/Hanshin rules, a 6-year-old who has not yet entered elementary school is classified as a "toddler" (yōji), not a school-age child (小児).
What this means: Ethan rides 100% FREE on all Osaka Metro subways, Kintetsu/Hanshin/JR trains, and Nankai Airport Express trains when traveling with you—you do not need to buy him an ICOCA card or subway tickets (just walk him through the wide manned/family ticket gate alongside you).
Exception (Nankai Rapi:t Limited Express): He rides free if sitting on your lap, or costs a half-price child ticket (¥750) if you book him his own reserved seat.
Your Payment Setup: Add a digital ICOCA or Suica to your Apple Wallet / Google Wallet before flying and load ~¥3,000–¥4,000 so you can tap through every subway and JR gate one-handed while holding Ethan's hand.
Stair-Free Luggage Elevators at Namba:
At Nankai Namba Station, follow signs for the South Exit / 1F Street Level Elevator (avoid underground Metro exits with stairs when rolling two 20 kg suitcases).
From Nankai Namba's street-level South/East side to Caption by Hyatt is a 400-meter (5–7 min) flat sidewalk walk straight east down Nansan-dori (なんさん通り) with zero stairs and only one pedestrian crossing at Sakaisuji Avenue.
